What this is

The Original Signal is a guided audio practice built around a single idea: most people who want a steadier prayer life do not stop because they stopped believing. They stop because the practice they were handed asked for more time than an ordinary week allows.

This one asks for a few minutes. You sit, you listen, you name what you are asking for, and you write one line before you get up. That is the whole method.

The four steps

  • Settle the room. Headphones, phone on airplane mode, hands open.
  • Open with words. A short prayer, spoken aloud, and one clear request.
  • Listen. A few minutes with your eyes closed, without forcing concentration.
  • Seal and note. Thirty seconds of silence, then one written line.
