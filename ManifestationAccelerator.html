What it is

The part most people skip

Reading a devotional and keeping one are two different things. Most people finish a study, feel something real, and then watch it fade over the following weeks — not from lack of sincerity, but because nothing carried it into the ordinary parts of the day.

The Accelerator is the bridge. It takes the twenty words from The Creation Key and puts them to work across seven structured days: a short scriptural focus each morning, a written reflection, and a spoken declaration for the evening.

It is a workbook, not another course. There is nothing new to learn — only a frame for practising what you already read.

“Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in your sight.”

Psalm 19:14

The seven days

How the week is built

Foundation. Naming what you are actually asking for — in plain words, written down, so the rest of the week has something to aim at.

Gratitude. A structured recall of what already stands, before asking for what does not yet.

Release. Working through what you have been carrying that no longer belongs to you.

Alignment. Matching the request to the life you are willing to live if it arrives.

Declaration. The words spoken aloud, morning and night, in your own voice.

Stillness. A quieter day — less speaking, more listening, with a guided reflection audio.

Sealing. Closing the week and deciding what carries forward into the next one.
